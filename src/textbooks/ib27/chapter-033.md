## Chapter 27 — 30-Second and 90-Second Pitches

### Learning objectives

- Deliver the project clearly at two lengths.
- Lead with question, finding, and limitation.
- Handle follow-up questions.

### Why it matters

Interviewers assess reasoning, not merely possession of files.

### Explanation

A dated 30-second pitch:

> I built an offline interview coach and a public-data merger teardown on Axcelis and Veeco. In the 2026-08-05/2026-08-06 frozen package, I tested whether the fixed 0.3575 all-stock ratio was reasonable using contribution analysis, six-peer comps, disclosed adviser fairness ranges, and an operating-proxy accretion model. The base case is accretive, but no synergy is dilutive, so the core risk is recovery and delivery of management’s $35 million annual run-rate guide rather than the last decimal of the ratio. I documented what the model cannot prove, including SAMR odds, final PPA, a bottoms-up synergy build, and a formal spread regression.

A 90-second version adds:

- source and date discipline;
- the `Merger!G73:G76` S-4 tie-out;
- the calculation comparing the $35 million annual run-rate guide with annualized Q1 2026 combined operating income;
- basic ownership;
- QA limits;
- the distinction between the 2026-08-05 market-data freeze and the 2026-08-06 QA run.

### Project connection

Synthetic audio files are practice-only and do not prove user mastery.

### Worked example

Follow-up: “What is your strongest finding?”  
Answer:

> In the 2026-08-05 frozen model, the $35 million annual run-rate guide is approximately 1.7 times annualized Q1 2026 combined operating income. That is a calculation from operating-income inputs, not a valuation conclusion, but it shows why the accretion case depends materially on recovery and execution.

### Common mistakes

- Starting with file counts.
- Saying “I proved the deal works.”
- Giving SAMR odds.
- Reading numbers without explaining implications.
- Omitting frozen-package dates.

### Checklist

- [ ] State question.
- [ ] State method.
- [ ] State finding.
- [ ] State limitation.
- [ ] Say the applicable frozen dates.
- [ ] Practice without notes.

### Takeaways

The best pitch is a compact evidence chain.
