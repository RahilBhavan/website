Sources checked for this edition on 25 October 2026. Standards and living documentation can change. Older reports provide frameworks and historical observations, not a current legal or market census. Chapter mathematics and fictional cases are original teaching constructions, not claims about the referenced projects' investment merits.

[1] ERC-20, Token Standard. Read the actual transfer, approval and allowance interface. https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-20

[2] ERC-721, Non-Fungible Token Standard. Read identifier ownership and transfer semantics. https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-721

[3] ERC-1155, Multi Token Standard. Read token-type and batch-operation structure. https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-1155

[4] ERC-4626, Tokenized Vaults. Read assets versus shares, previews, limits, fees and rounding. https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-4626

[5] Ethereum documentation, Proof of stake. Read its chain-specific security model; do not generalize its parameters to every token. https://ethereum.org/en/developers/docs/consensus-mechanisms/pos/

[6] Uniswap v2 whitepaper, 2020. Read constant-product exchange mechanics and compare assumptions with Chapter 5. https://app.uniswap.org/whitepaper.pdf

[7] Federal Reserve staff research, Tokenization: Overview and Financial Stability Implications, 2023, revised December 2023. A research framework, not an approval of any offering. https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/feds/tokenization-overview-and-financial-stability-implications.htm

[8] BIS Annual Economic Report 2023, Chapter III, Blueprint for the future monetary system. Read tokenization and settlement architecture as a proposed design framework. https://www.bis.org/publ/arpdf/ar2023e3.htm

[9] ERC-3643, T-REX, Token for Regulated EXchanges. Read permissioned-token interfaces and identify dependencies outside the contract. https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-3643

[10] OpenZeppelin Contracts 5.x documentation, ERC-4626. Read vault inflation attacks and implementation considerations. https://docs.openzeppelin.com/contracts/5.x/erc4626

[11] Financial Stability Board, The Financial Stability Implications of Tokenisation, 22 October 2024. Read the liquidity, leverage, asset-quality, interconnectedness and operational-risk framework. https://www.fsb.org/2024/10/the-financial-stability-implications-of-tokenisation/

[12] Franklin OnChain U.S. Government Money Fund prospectus, filed 25 July 2025, dated 1 August 2025. A dated issuer disclosure filed with the SEC, not SEC endorsement. https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1786958/000174177325002737/c485bpos.htm

Reading protocol

For every real product, record source title, issuer, document version, observation date, exact claim, supporting passage and unresolved questions. A current webpage can discuss an old event. Preserve publication date separately from access date. Verify deployed addresses and chain rather than relying on names or symbols. Stop when the next conclusion requires evidence you do not have, then name that evidence explicitly.

[16] Franklin OnChain U.S. Government Money Fund: summary prospectus, 1 August 2026. Read with the full document and relevant supplements.

[13] EIP-1559. Base-fee mechanism. Historical terminology needs chain-version context.

[14] BIS Annual Economic Report 2025, Chapter III. Programmable-platform architecture; a design framework, not a legal approval.
