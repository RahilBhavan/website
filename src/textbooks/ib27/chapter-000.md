This book explains how IB27 turns registered public evidence into a bounded semiconductor merger judgment while supporting an offline investment-banking interview coach.

### Evidence labels

- **Verified fact:** Supported directly by a filing, workbook cell, repository record, test result, or source register.
- **Calculation:** Arithmetic derived from verified inputs.
- **Analyst assumption:** A selected forecast, scenario, probability, tax rate, or normalization choice.
- **Analyst judgment:** An interpretation or recommendation framing.
- **Illustration:** Educational arithmetic or example that is not a project output.
- **Unresolved gap:** Information unavailable in the frozen public-data scope or requiring human review.

### Central question

> Was 0.3575 Axcelis shares per Veeco share a reasonable exchange ratio, and under the stated assumptions does the pro forma company appear attractive to own?

The project analyzes an all-stock Axcelis Technologies/Veeco Instruments transaction. It does not provide investment advice, a SAMR approval forecast, a complete PPA, a bottoms-up synergy build, a formal event study, or proof of authentic speaking mastery.
