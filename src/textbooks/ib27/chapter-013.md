## Chapter 10 — VECO and Adjacent Process Capabilities

### Learning objectives

- Explain VECO’s disclosed equipment categories.
- Understand complementarity without implying interchangeability.
- Connect portfolio breadth to merger logic.

### Why it matters

“Strategic fit” is useful only when defined operationally.

### Explanation

VECO’s disclosed capabilities include laser annealing, ion-beam deposition, MOCVD, and single-wafer etch and clean.

The project interprets VECO as adding adjacent process capabilities to ACLS’s implant franchise. This is an **analyst judgment**. The project does not establish that products are integrated, interchangeable, or automatically cross-sold.

### Project connection

The broader portfolio may improve commercial reach and service breadth. These are **unquantified judgments**; no customer-overlap, qualification, or revenue-synergy evidence was built.

### Worked example — Illustration

```text
ACLS: implantation breadth
+
VECO: annealing, deposition, MOCVD, etch, clean
=
broader equipment portfolio
```

This illustration does not establish revenue synergy or product integration.

### Common mistakes

- Calling the combination an end-to-end fab platform.
- Claiming specific process-node wins.
- Treating adjacent tools as substitutes.
- Calling commercial reach or service breadth measured outputs.

### Checklist

- [ ] Use categories supported by filings.
- [ ] Separate fact from strategic judgment.
- [ ] State missing qualification evidence.
- [ ] Do not imply quantified cross-selling.

### Takeaways

VECO adds adjacency and breadth, not proven automatic commercial synergies.
