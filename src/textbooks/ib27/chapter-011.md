## Chapter 8 — Industry Overview

### Learning objectives

- Explain why semiconductor equipment is cyclical.
- Distinguish equipment categories from chip products.
- Connect industry cycles to modeling.

### Why it matters

Equipment revenue depends on customer fab spending, not only end-market demand.

### Explanation

Semiconductor manufacturing uses specialized deposition, patterning, etch, clean, implantation, annealing, inspection, and metrology tools. Fab spending can be delayed by inventory, utilization, financing, technology transitions, or regional restrictions.

### Project connection

The project uses FY2018–FY2025 median operating margins as **one selected broad-cycle window**. This is an analyst judgment, not a validated full-cycle normalization; the repository explicitly identifies the one-cycle limitation.

### Common mistakes

- Treating semiconductor demand as linear.
- Equating AI growth with guaranteed ACLS or VECO revenue.
- Claiming node-specific exposure without evidence.
- Calling one broad-cycle window a complete cycle.
