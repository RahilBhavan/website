Chapter 0

How to use this book

After this chapter you can
  • Choose a four-week path that ends before 15 November.
  • Tell a worked number from an assumption.

This book teaches the Mount Meru CTRM: the business it models, the maths behind it, and the rules the code follows. It is a study aid. The specification, the ADRs and the code stay the source. When this book and one of them disagree, they win.

The company buys crude soybean oil in Argentina, ships it to Beira, stores it, and trucks it inland. It handles about 200,000 MT a year, worth about $300M. The CTRM records price, cargo and cash for each tonne. Odoo keeps the accounts.

How each chapter works

  • Goals first. Then the idea, a picture, a worked example, and a short check.
  • Every number is computed by docs/textbook/calc.py and checked against the golden cases before this page is built.
  • ILLUSTRATION marks a picture with toy or spec-example numbers. They are not Mount Meru's live data.
  • NOT CONFIRMED marks a rule the spec lists as "to confirm" with the client. Treat it as an assumption until docs/client-questions.md says otherwise.
Three words you must keep apart

Price is what a tonne costs. Cargo is where the tonne is. Cash is how and when it is paid. The whole system is built so these three stay separate and can still be added up.

What this book does not claim

It does not say the system is ready for go-live, that any provider is connected, or that any answer from the client is final. Check docs/handoff/ and docs/client-questions.md for the current state.

Chapter 1

The business: one tonne's journey

After this chapter you can
  • Trace a tonne from the load port to the refinery gate.
  • Name the six kinds of risk and where each one shows up.
  • Say why Odoo, not the CTRM, keeps the accounts.

A term contract with a large trader commits Mount Meru to buy, for example, 30,000 MT over six months. That is 5,000 MT a month. Each month's oil loads onto a tanker at San Lorenzo near Rosario. It sails to Beira. A pipeline moves it into Mount Meru's terminal. Road tankers of about 32 MT then carry it to a Mount Meru refinery (an internal transfer) or to a third-party refiner (a sale).

Every physical step has a money step. The gaps in time between them are where Mount Meru carries risk.

Where the risk sits

RiskWhat it isHow the CTRM shows it
Flat-priceCBOT futures move before a lot is fixed, or after it is fixed but before the oil is soldUnmatched exposure and its sensitivity per USc/lb; daily MTM
BasisThe Argentine premium over CBOT moves on its ownBasis part of MTM, shown apart from the futures part
QuantityOil is lost in transit; contract toleranceTransit-loss report; shipped vs planned vs priced per period
FundingLC maturities cluster while customers pay lateLC maturity ladder against receivables and cash
CounterpartyA customer does not pay, or a supplier does not loadReceivables ageing from Odoo; fulfilment status
LogisticsVessel delays; trucks not registered at the portVessel ETA against contract dates; nomination status

The scale

At September 2026 prices CBOT soybean oil is near 68 USc/lb, about $1,502 per tonne before basis. Two sums to keep in mind:

  • Whole-year exposure per cent: 200,000 MT x 22.0462 = $4,409,240.00 per USc/lb. This overstates risk. Only unpriced tonnes are exposed.
  • A typical month of unpriced volume: 16,700 MT x 22.0462 = about $368k per USc/lb.
Odoo stays the system of record

The CTRM owns trade, pricing, inventory and risk. It posts to Odoo (partners, invoices, bills) and reads balances back. It does not rebuild what Odoo already does. Inventory value in the CTRM is an operating cost measure, not statutory accounting.

Not confirmed yet

The Argentine basis level is not yet confirmed with the client (question Q-01 in the spec, CQ-01 in the log). The sizes above use 68 USc/lb with basis 0 as a stand-in.

Check yourself

  1. Why is $4.4M per USc/lb the wrong risk number to quote?

    Answer

    It assumes all 200,000 MT a year is unpriced at once. Risk exists only on tonnes whose futures price is still open, and less still after matching sales.

  2. Who keeps the invoice and payment accounts?

    Answer

    Odoo. The CTRM stores the Odoo reference and pushes or reads through an adapter.
