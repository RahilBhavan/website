A note on numbers
Every concrete number in this textbook is annotated with its source. Where a number was derived (e.g. ridge points, arithmetic intensities), the formula is shown. Where it was measured by a third party, the source is named. Re-verify hardware claims against the latest NVIDIA whitepapers and product pages before publishing or quoting them in production work. Architectures and metric names drift between toolkit versions; the structure of the analysis is the durable part.
