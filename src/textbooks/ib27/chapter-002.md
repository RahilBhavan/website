## Chapter 1 — The Project in Plain English

### Learning objectives

- Explain the two products in IB27.
- State the transaction question.
- Separate facts, calculations, assumptions, judgments, and gaps.

### Why it matters

Strong analysts know what a project is actually trying to answer. Scope prevents attractive but irrelevant analysis from replacing decision-useful work.

### Explanation

IB27 combines:

1. An offline interview-coaching SPA containing lessons, flashcards, quizzes, mock prompts, deadlines, CRM tools, merger math, and project notes.
2. A public-data transaction teardown covering Axcelis and Veeco.

The transaction terms include:

- Fixed exchange ratio: **0.3575 ACLS shares per VECO share**.
- Announced equity value: approximately **$2,130.45 million**, using announcement-date inputs.
- Basic ownership: approximately **58.48% ACLS / 41.52% VECO**.
- Management’s **$35 million annual run-rate synergy guide**, targeted within 24 months with the majority expected in the first 12 months. The registered source does not establish whether the guide is pretax or after-tax.
- SAMR was reported as the remaining condition in the frozen materials, **as of the applicable registered source date**.

**Sources/as-of:** `PROJECT.md`; `research/09_qa/claim_ledger.md`; applicable registered transaction materials; workbook market-data freeze 2026-08-05.

### Common mistakes

- Calling a modeled assumption a fact.
- Treating the spread as a recommendation.
- Calling the workbook a complete valuation.
- Describing the synergy guide without its annual run-rate, timing, tax-status, and source qualifications.
- Ignoring source dates.

### Checklist

- [ ] State the question before collecting data.
- [ ] Label every material claim.
- [ ] Record the applicable as-of date.
- [ ] State what the project does not prove.
- [ ] Distinguish management’s annual run-rate guide from realized savings.

### Takeaways

1. Scope is an analytical control.
2. The workbook is authoritative for model economics.
3. The $35 million comparison is a dated calculation, not a valuation conclusion.
4. Automated QA is evidence, not final human approval.
