Median, CI, and Coefficient of Variation

statistics
  • Warm up for 50 launches; discard those samples.
  • Sample N ≥ 200 launches.
  • Report the median, not the mean — kernel launches have a heavy right tail.
  • Coefficient of variation (CV = stdev/mean) should be < 2%. Higher means your measurement is contaminated by another process, thermal throttling, or driver scheduling. Stop and fix the rig before publishing.
  • 95% confidence interval via percentile or bootstrap. "Median 38.4 µs (95% CI: 38.1–38.7)" is a real claim. "Mean 41 µs" is not.
  • Paired test when comparing two kernels — alternate them launch-by-launch to absorb shared environmental noise. Then use the per-pair difference for the CI.
