Check yourself

  1. Why can inventory not import models from pricing?

    Answer

    Modules may import only another module's services.py and selectors.py. CI fails the build otherwise (ADR-0001).

  2. The Databento feed is down. Can traders still value positions?

    Answer

    Yes. They enter marks manually and a second person approves (ADR-0003).

  3. A POST times out. The trader retries. Why is there no duplicate?

    Answer

    The retry carries the same Idempotency-Key, so the server recognises the original request.

Practice

After this chapter you can
  • Check your numbers against worked answers.

Try each problem on paper first. Use 22.0462 for the conversion. Then open the answer. All answers were computed by the same code that checks the golden cases.

  1. Convert a basis of +1.25 USc/lb to USD/MT.

    Worked answer

    1.25 x 22.0462 = 27.5578.

  2. Lots: 1,500 MT at 70.00 and 500 MT at 72.00 USc/lb, basis +1.00. Find F avg and P avg.

    Worked answer

    F avg = (1500x70 + 500x72)/2000 = 70.5. P avg = (70.5 + 1.00) x 22.0462 = 1,576.3033 USD/MT.

  3. A purchase of 2,000 MT fixed at F avg 70.50, basis 1.00. Today F 71.00, basis 0.50. Find the futures part, basis part and total.

    Worked answer

    Futures: 2000 x 0.50 x 22.0462 = +22,046.20. Basis: 2000 x (−0.50) x 22.0462 = −22,046.20. Total = +0.00. The parts cancel.

  4. Same as problem 3 but it is a sale. What is the total MTM?

    Worked answer

    Zero again, with both parts flipped in sign.

  5. 5,000 MT is priced and the B/L is 4,800 MT. How much is over-priced, and what is its sensitivity?

    Worked answer

    200 MT. 200 x 22.0462 = 4,409.24 USD per USc/lb.

  6. A period plans 6,000 MT at ±5% and 6,100 MT is already priced. Can a 200 MT lot be fixed?

    Worked answer

    q_max = 6,300. 6,100 + 200 = 6,300, which is not above q_max, so yes. One more tonne would be refused.

  7. Unpriced purchases are 5,000 MT and unpriced CBOT-linked sales are 2,000 MT. Sensitivity?

    Worked answer

    3,000 MT x 22.0462 = 66,138.60 USD per USc/lb.

  8. TWAP over a window is 68.00. You bought 4,000 MT at an average 67.60. Execution score?

    Worked answer

    (68.00 − 67.60) x 22.0462 x 4,000 = +35,273.92 USD. You beat even spreading.

  9. 4,975 MT discharged from a 5,000 MT B/L. Loss, percent, and landed cost per tonne if landed cost is $8,000,000.

    Worked answer

    25 MT, 0.50%. Per tonne at the depot = 8,000,000 / 4,975 = 1,608.04.

  10. Depot: 1,000 MT at $1,600. Receipt: 2,000 MT at $1,630. New weighted average?

    Worked answer

    (1000x1600 + 2000x1630)/3000 = 1,620.00.

  11. LC amount $5,000,000, 8% a year, 90 days (on a 360-day basis). Financing cost?

    Worked answer

    5,000,000 x 0.08 x 90/360 = 100,000.00 USD.

  12. Plan trucks for 700 MT at 32 MT per truck.

    Worked answer

    ceil(700/32) = 22 trucks. Last truck = 700 − 32 x 21 = 28 MT.
