The technical writeup

Format: a single blog post or Twitter thread. Lead with the roofline plot — that picture tells the entire story in one frame. Follow with the table. Then 4–6 paragraphs walking through the kernel-by-kernel optimizations, focusing on the bottleneck each one solved (Nsight Compute metric → code change → measured delta). Close with a falsifiable claim, e.g. "On RTX 3090 with locked clocks, M = N = K = 4096 FP16 GEMM, my kernel sustains 130 TFLOPS vs 125 TFLOPS for cuBLAS 12.2, verified across 500 iterations."

What "reproducible" actually means
A stranger should be able to clone your repo, run one script, and within ten minutes have a table on their console that they can compare against yours. If they can't — if there are manual steps, magic environment variables, or missing baseline runs — the result is unverifiable and will be discounted, regardless of how good your kernel is.
