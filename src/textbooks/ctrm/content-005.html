Chapter 10

Sales, nominations and trucks

After this chapter you can
  • Follow a customer request to a delivered truck.
  • Plan trucks for a quantity.

Local customers collect oil from the depot by truck. A sales order records the customer and quantity. A nomination is the request to collect a stated quantity, submitted to the port ahead of loading. Trucks cannot enter the terminal until registered.

Notifications and statements

On loaded, departed and delivered, nominated contacts can receive a message. Messaging has manual and stub paths. Customer statements show what was nominated, loaded and delivered, with invoice references from Odoo. Bank reconciliation of customer payments stays in Odoo.

Known seam

A sales order has no implemented foreign-key link to a term sale contract. Vessel shipments are purchase-only. Do not assume recording one updates the other.

Architecture and rules

After this chapter you can

  • State the module shape and the import rule.

These are accepted decisions, not preferences. Changing one means a new ADR, not an edit to code.

ADRRuleWhy it exists
0001Modular monolith: one repository, one PostgreSQL database, Django and DRF as web plus worker. No microservices, Kafka, Kubernetes or separate pricing service.Simplest design a small team can own
0002Refine + Ant Design for high-value operational screens; Django Admin + Unfold for master data and queues. Never build a CRUD screen twice.Spend front-end effort where it pays
0003Every external system sits behind an interface with a manual fallback. Odoo model names, Databento symbols and AIS fields never appear in contracts, pricing, inventory, lc or sales.A feed being down never stops the product

ADR-0005 explains how status lives on append-only tables: decisions are their own rows and the selector derives status. ADR-0006 (proposed) sets shipments to call inventory.

Rules every endpoint follows

  • Actions, not edits. State changes are actions such as POST /api/v1/shipments/{id}/actions/mark-loaded. Each is a django-fsm-2 transition with a permission check and an audit entry.
  • Idempotency. Every write accepts an Idempotency-Key header. A retry after a lost response must not create a duplicate.
  • Entity scoping. Every query is scoped by legal entity through one shared queryset method, and fails closed.
  • Maker-checker. Independent decisions for contracts, lots, marks, adjustments, access changes.
  • Roles. Trader, Operations, Treasury, Finance, Management, Admin. A capability says what you may do; an entity grant says whose records you may see.
  • Audit. pghistory records changes; GET /api/v1/audit/ reads them.

Time

The business zone is Africa/Maputo. API timestamps are UTC. The MTM run, snapshot and report dates use the CBOT trade date. Three questions for any figure: which business day, which pricing date, and what was known when it was built.
