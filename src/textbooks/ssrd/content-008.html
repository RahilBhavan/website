Glossary

ASN
Autonomous System Number: the ID of a network operator on the internet. Your building's ISP is AS22768. Used for the egress check, and as a weak (D) signal.
Base rate
How common the thing you're looking for is in the population you test. It sets how far false positives drag precision down.
Checkpoint
A CT log's signed statement of its current size and root hash.
Cloaking / decoy
Showing a harmless page to visitors who don't look like victims. Labeled decoy-cloaked; never evaded.
Containment
Shared items ÷ the size of the smaller set. Used for the copy fingerprint.
CT (certificate transparency)
Public, append-only logs of TLS certificates. SSRD's source of newly certified names.
DNS rebinding
Making a name resolve to a public address when it's checked and a private one when it's used.
eTLD+1 / registrable domain
The public suffix plus one label, such as quantum-invest.co.uk. The unit SSRD dedupes and visits.
Family (A/B/C/D)
A group of signals that measure the same kind of thing: content, assets, page features, infrastructure.
Jaccard similarity
|A ∩ B| ÷ |A ∪ B| for two sets.
Kappa (Cohen's κ)
Agreement between two labelers beyond what chance alone would give.
Kit
Reused scam-site software (templates, scripts, copy). A kit match means the same software, never the same people.
MinHash
A short signature whose agreement rate estimates Jaccard similarity.
mmh3
MurmurHash3, a fast non-cryptographic hash. Used for favicons (Shodan recipe), n-gram buckets and copy shingles.
Names tile
A compact JSONL tile listing just the DNS names of 256 log entries. A non-core extension some static-CT logs serve.
p90
The value 90% of observations fall at or below.
Pass rate
The share of all new names the filter sends to the fetch queue. A measure of workload.
Precertificate
A logged copy of a certificate, made before issuance so proofs can be embedded. It's why CT entries can come before the site.
Precision / recall
Precision: the share of alerts that are real. Recall: the share of real cases that alert.
PSL
Public Suffix List: where registrable names begin, including private suffixes such as vercel.app.
Punycode (xn--)
The ASCII encoding of internationalized domain labels.
Shingle
A run of k consecutive items (tags or words) used to compare documents as sets.
SSRF
Server-side request forgery: tricking a fetcher into requesting internal addresses.
Static CT / tiles
Logs published as cacheable files in 256-entry tiles instead of an API.
TLSH
Trend Micro locality-sensitive hash. Similar inputs give hashes with a small distance.
Union-find
A data structure that tracks which items are in the same group as links are added.
Wilson interval
A confidence interval for a proportion that behaves well at small n and near 0% or 100%.
