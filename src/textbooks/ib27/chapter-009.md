## Chapter 7 — Forecasting and Merger Accretion

### Learning objectives

- Build assumption-driven forecasts.
- Calculate share issuance, EPS, accretion, and breakeven synergy.
- Explain operating-proxy limitations.

### Why it matters

An accretion result can look precise while resting on uncertain recovery, PPA, shares, and synergy assumptions.

### Explanation

Forecast formulas:

\[
Revenue_t=Revenue_{t-1}(1+g_t)
\]

\[
GP_t=GP_{t-1}+(Revenue_t-Revenue_{t-1})\times IGM
\]

\[
OI=GP-R\&D-SG\&A
\]

\[
EPS=NI/Diluted\ shares
\]

Transaction mechanics:

\[
Issued\ shares=VECO\ shares\times0.3575
\]

\[
PF\ shares=ACLS\ shares+Issued\ shares
\]

\[
Accretion=PF\ EPS/ACLS\ EPS-1
\]

Frozen operating-proxy outputs:

| Case | FY2026E accretion | FY2027E accretion |
|---|---:|---:|
| No synergy | −7.71% | −10.70% |
| Base synergy | +6.52% | +2.24% |
| Full synergy | +20.75% | +15.18% |

Breakeven synergy is approximately $9.482 million in the frozen FY2026E operating-proxy case.

The $35 million guide is an annual run-rate target within 24 months, with the majority expected in the first 12 months. The registered source does not establish whether it is pretax or after-tax.

### Project connection

The base case is accretive, but the no-synergy case is dilutive. PPA drag and costs-to-achieve are excluded from the headline operating-proxy bridge; full diluted-share treatment and financing effects are not complete.

### Worked example — Illustration

If standalone EPS is $2.00 and pro forma EPS is $2.13:

\[
2.13/2.00-1=6.5\%
\]

### Common mistakes

- Treating management guidance as realized synergy.
- Calling the model GAAP-complete.
- Ignoring final diluted shares.
- Treating financing and PPA items as already resolved.
- Giving SAMR a probability without evidence.

### Checklist

- [ ] Assumptions labeled.
- [ ] Cases visible.
- [ ] Shares and tax effects included where modeled.
- [ ] PPA and costs-to-achieve exclusions stated.
- [ ] Excel recalculation remains a human gate.

### Takeaways

Accretion is a conditional output, not an investment conclusion by itself.
